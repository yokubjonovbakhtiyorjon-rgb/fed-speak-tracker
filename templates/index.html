Sources and method

Board items come from the Board of Governors' official RSS feed. Reserve Bank items come from each Bank's own feed or speech listing where available, and from Fed in Print, the Federal Reserve System's publication index maintained by the St. Louis Fed. When the same remarks appear in more than one source, the most primary source is linked first and the others are listed as alternates. Topic tags are keyword-based aids and do not characterize a policy stance. Media interviews without an official transcript are generally not captured.
